Admissions

Join a school where learning is meaningful.

We welcome students and families who value quality education, discipline, character and growth. Our admissions process is simple, transparent and student-focused.

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • Students seeking quality education from Grade 1 onward
  • Families seeking a disciplined, supportive school environment
  • Students with a willingness to learn and participate
Process

How to apply

  • Submit the admission inquiry form
  • Complete the document verification step
  • Meet with the school administration
Requirements

Documents needed

  • Birth certificate / citizenship copy
  • Previous academic records
  • Passport-size photographs

Begin your application today

Contact the school office for admission dates, class availability and the latest updates.
